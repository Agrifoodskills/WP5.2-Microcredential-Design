--- slide
id: wp52-05
layout: Split
title: Map teaching and workplace learning as one coherent route
left:
  type: ul
  items:
    - "**Teaching:** no more than three stackable units"
    - Define the knowledge, decision, or skill each unit enables
    - Identify what a learner needs before workplace application
right:
  type: ul
  items:
    - "**Workplace-based learning:** no more than three stackable units"
    - Define the authentic activity and evidence for each unit
    - Show how the six units stack towards the final award
notes: |
  Give partners two minutes to sketch the outline map of their two
  courses. Ask them to draw connections between teaching and workplace
  units, rather than listing the two strands separately.

  Prompt them to use EUVETMC for each module and to flag whether the
  final award should use EUVETMC, EUVETD, or C.

--- slide
id: wp52-06
layout: Comparison
title: "**Design for adult learners**"
left_head: Pedagogy
left:
  type: ul
  items:
    - The teacher decides what content comes first
    - Learning is separated from its use
    - Motivation is mainly external
right_head: Andragogy
right:
  type: ul
  items:
    - The learner brings a workplace problem worth solving
    - Learning and workplace application reinforce each other
    - Motivation comes from better practice and professional progress
notes: |
  Ask partners to name one workplace problem their first course should
  help learners solve. That problem should shape the teaching, WBL, and
  assessment route.

  Close by asking each partner to nominate an owner and date for their
  first two-course outline map.
